The house

Bonnie's Nest Too is where we go to slow down. It sits at the end of a quiet road in the foothills southwest of Reno, with sagebrush out front and the Sierra Nevada filling every west-facing window. The great room has a two-story stone fireplace, a long table that seats ten, and a kitchen big enough for two cooks who don't agree.

Four bedrooms sleep eight comfortably: a king suite on the main floor, two queens upstairs, and a bunk room the kids fight over. Out back there's a hot tub, a gas fire pit, and a deck you'll end up on every evening. In winter, Mt. Rose ski resort is 30 minutes away; in summer, Lake Tahoe's beaches are about 45.
