About Bonnie

Homes we actually live in

We didn't set out to be in the rental business. We bought the Puerto Vallarta house for winters and the Reno house for summers, and somewhere along the way friends started asking if they could borrow them. Then friends of friends.

So here we are. When we're not in one, it's yours. Every home is stocked the way we'd want it for our own family: good knives, real coffee, extra towels, and a binder of the places we'd send you first.
